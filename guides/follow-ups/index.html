Follow-Ups That Don't Slip: Customers and Candidates

Follow-ups slip because nobody owns the reminder. Here is how sales and people teams keep customers and candidates from waiting.

Why do follow-ups slip?

Because they are small, they are urgent only later, and they compete with everything else in the inbox. By the time someone notices, the customer or candidate has been waiting.

How does this work for sales?

After a customer call, Jin drafts the follow-up from the conversation and the thread before it. When a customer thread goes quiet, it prepares a reply to restart it. The rep reviews and sends.

How does this work for people teams?

When a candidate reply is pending, Jin drafts the follow-up so it is ready to approve. Scheduling messages for interview loops can be prepared the same way.

What should I check before sending?

Names, dates, and commitments. Then adjust the tone if needed. A quick edit is better than a rewrite, because it shows Jin what to keep.

Pro tip: If a draft is not needed, skip it. That tells Jin which threads matter.

Who stays in control?

You do. Jin prepares drafts and a person reviews and sends them. Nothing is sent or changed without approval.

FAQ

Will Jin email customers on its own?

No. Jin prepares drafts. A person reviews and sends them.

Which tools does it work with?

Jin works with the email, calendar, chat, and files your team connects, such as Gmail, Outlook, Slack, and Microsoft Teams.
